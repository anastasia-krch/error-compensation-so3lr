# Chapter map

1. `01_density` — density versus temperature.
2. `02_rdf` — nitrogen-based anion--anion, cation--cation, and cation--anion RDFs.
3. `03_ion_pair_correlations` — intermittent nearest-neighbour ion-pair correlations.
4. `04_hydrogen_bonds` — all-ring-C--H RDFs against O and F of NTf2-.
5. `05_transport` — diffusion, Green--Kubo conductivity, Nernst--Einstein conductivity, and Haven ratio.
6. `06_dihedrals` — NTf2 backbone C--S--N--S/S--N--S--C distributions.
7. `07_charges` — SO3LR partial charges: environment response, dipole control, and the gas-phase dissociation curve.

Simulation setup is in `simulations/`, with separate APPLE&P/AMS and SO3LR
NPT -> NVT -> NVE templates.
